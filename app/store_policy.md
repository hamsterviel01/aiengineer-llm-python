# Store Policy

This document describes the official policies of our online store. Customer service answers must be consistent with these policies.

## Shipping

We ship to all 50 US states, Canada, the United Kingdom, and all countries in the European Union. We do not currently ship to other countries.

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days within the United States. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on business days and arrives the next business day.

Standard shipping costs $5.99 per order. Express shipping costs $14.99 and overnight shipping costs $29.99. Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 within the United States.

International shipping to Canada takes 7 to 10 business days and costs $15. International shipping to the United Kingdom and the European Union takes 10 to 14 business days and costs $25. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by the destination country.

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. Once an order ships, customers receive a confirmation email with a tracking number.

## Order Tracking

Customers can track their order by logging into their account and opening the Orders page, or by using the tracking link in the shipping confirmation email. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to appear after the order ships.

If a package shows as delivered but has not arrived, customers should wait 48 hours, check with neighbors, and then contact customer support. We will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Order Changes and Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled or changed within 1 hour of being placed, as long as they have not yet been processed. To cancel, customers can open the order in their account and select Cancel Order. After 1 hour, or once the order has been processed, it can no longer be cancelled, but the customer may return the items after delivery.

The shipping address can be changed within 1 hour of placing the order. After that, customers must contact customer support, and we cannot guarantee the change.

## Returns

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all tags attached.

The following items cannot be returned: gift cards, downloadable software, personalized or engraved items, underwear and swimwear, and perishable goods.

To start a return, customers log into their account, open the order, and select Return Items. We email a prepaid return shipping label. Return shipping is free for customers in the United States. International customers pay their own return shipping costs.

Electronics can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Opened electronics are subject to a 15% restocking fee unless they are defective.

## Exchanges

Customers can exchange an item for a different size or color within 30 days of delivery. Exchanges are free within the United States. If the requested size or color is out of stock, we issue a refund instead.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Depending on the bank, it can take an additional 3 to 5 business days for the refund to appear on the customer's statement.

Original shipping costs are not refunded, except when the item was defective or we sent the wrong item. Orders paid with store credit are refunded as store credit.

## Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, or if we sent the wrong item, customers should contact customer support within 7 days of delivery and include photos. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including shipping costs. Customers do not need to return damaged items unless we request it.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency.

Buy now, pay later is available through Klarna for orders between $35 and $1,000. Klarna splits the total into 4 interest-free payments made every 2 weeks.

Payment is charged when the order is placed. Prices are shown in US dollars. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address.

## Promo Codes and Discounts

Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to orders that have already been placed. Promo codes cannot be combined with other offers unless stated otherwise.

If a promo code does not work, customers should check the expiration date, the minimum order amount, and whether the items in the cart are eligible.

New customers who sign up for our newsletter receive a 10% discount code for their first order.

## Price Adjustments

If an item's price drops within 14 days of purchase, customers can contact customer support to receive a refund of the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance items or flash sales.

## Warranty

Electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. Our own branded products come with a 2-year warranty. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear. To make a warranty claim, customers contact customer support with the order number and a description of the problem.

## Accounts

Customers can reset their password by selecting Forgot Password on the login page. A reset link is sent by email and expires after 24 hours.

Customers can unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email or by changing their preferences in the account settings. Order and shipping emails cannot be turned off.

Customers can request deletion of their account by contacting customer support. Account deletion is completed within 30 days.

## Store Pickup

Customers can choose free in-store pickup at checkout. Orders are usually ready for pickup within 2 hours during store opening hours. Orders are held for 7 days. If an order is not picked up within 7 days, it is cancelled and refunded.

## Product Availability

Out-of-stock items show a Notify Me button. Customers who select it receive an email when the item is back in stock. We cannot guarantee restock dates.

## Customer Support

Customer support is available by live chat and email 7 days a week, from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time. Phone support is available Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. We reply to emails within 24 hours.

Customer support will never ask for a full credit card number or a password.
